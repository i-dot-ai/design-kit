## About the i.AI Design Kit

The i.AI Design Kit is intended for anyone designing or building products for the [Incubator for Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.gov.uk/).

It extends the [GOV.UK Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/) with new styles, components and patterns, and supports React, Svelte, Solid.js and Web Components.

## Contact us

The i.AI DesignKit is maintained by i.AI staff. You can contact us in the [#iai-design-kit](https://i-dot-ai.slack.com/archives/C0C476E1D4H) Slack channel, or find out how to [contribute to the Kit](https://design-kit-prototype-c62ad06bf5a5.herokuapp.com/get-started/kit-forum).
