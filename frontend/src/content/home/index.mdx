## About the i.AI Design Kit

The i.AI Design Kit helps you design and build products which have a consistent style and branding, designed for accessibility.

It's based on the [GOV.UK Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/) and adds styles, components and patterns relevant for i.AI. It works with React, Svelte, Solid.js and Web Components.

## Contact us

i.AI manage and maintain the i.AI Design Kit. If you have any questions you can contact us in the [#iai-design-kit](https://i-dot-ai.slack.com/archives/C0C476E1D4H) Slack channel, or [suggest a contribution](/get-started/contribute-to-the-design-kit).
