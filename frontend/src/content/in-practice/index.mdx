These are stories of how i.AI product teams have used the Design Kit: what they built, where the kit helped, where they had to go further, and what they learned.

Each story is short. It shows the product changing over time, lets you try it, and links to the team's own design history for the detail.

Use them to see how the kit's components, styles and patterns work together in a real product, and to learn from the gaps other teams found.
