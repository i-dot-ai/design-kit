The patterns in this section are solutions to pain points that our products have come 
up against when trying to align to GOV.UK Frontend.

In line with [our decision to only extend the GOV.UK Design System where necessary](/principles/decision-record#3-well-only-extend-the-govuk-design-system-where-necessary), 
the solutions and patterns listed here are to be applied when those pain points are 
experienced in your products and not as a starting point.

Each pattern is listed under the pain point it was designed to solve. If you have a 
problem which is not covered here, or a new solution then please [share your problems 
or solutions with the Design Kit](/get-started/contribute-to-the-design-kit).
