## Default

The GOV.UK footer sits at the bottom of every page. It carries the Crown logo, the
Open Government Licence statement and the Crown copyright notice, and can hold
navigation links to other parts of the service.

## When to use this component

Use the GOV.UK footer on GOV.UK-branded products and on departmental products.
Products are branded either as GOV.UK or as a government department depending on
their audience — see [Products are branded either GOV.UK or a government
department](/principles/decision-record#1-well-brand-products-according-to-the-needs-of-their-users). Both brands use this
footer:

- **GOV.UK brand** — use the GOV.UK footer alongside the [GOV.UK
  header](/components/govuk-header) when the product is public-facing or used across
  the public sector.
- **Departmental brand** — use the GOV.UK footer alongside the [generic
  header](/components/header).

The Crown logo and Open Government Licence are part of the GOV.UK brand — see [Brand
is the product name, header, font, brand colour and
logo](/principles/decision-record).

## When not to use this component

Do not use the GOV.UK footer to present an i.AI corporate identity. The i.AI brand
is a corporate brand and is not applied to product interfaces — see [The i.AI brand
is a corporate brand and is not applied to product
interfaces](/principles/decision-record).

Do not use the Crown logo, the Open Government Licence or the "© Crown copyright"
statement on a product that is not entitled to them. If your product must not
identify itself as GOV.UK, follow the [Service Manual guidance on making your service
look like
GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-your-service-look-like-govuk)
and remove or replace these elements.
