## Default

The GOV.UK header carries the Crown logotype and the GOV.UK wordmark. Set a
`productName` to show the name of your service alongside it.

## When to use this component

Use the GOV.UK header only when the product is branded as GOV.UK — that is, when it
is public-facing or used across the public sector. Products are branded either as
GOV.UK or as a government department depending on their audience — see [Products are
branded either GOV.UK or a government
department](/decisions-record/branding-determined-by-audience).

The header is one of the elements that carries the brand — see [Brand is the product
name, header, font, brand colour and
logo](/decisions-record/brand-is-header-font-colour-logo). Using the GOV.UK header,
the GDS Transport typeface and the GOV.UK colours together is what makes a product
read as GOV.UK.

## When not to use this component

Do not use the GOV.UK header on a product that is not part of GOV.UK. The [Service
Manual guidance on making your service look like
GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-your-service-look-like-govuk)
states that a product which is not GOV.UK must not identify itself as part of
GOV.UK or use the Crown or GOV.UK logotype in the header — see [Brand is the product
name, header, font, brand colour and
logo](/decisions-record/brand-is-header-font-colour-logo).

For a departmental or i.AI-defaulted product, use the [Generic header](/components/header)
instead. Do not use the GOV.UK header to present an i.AI corporate identity — the
i.AI brand is not applied to product interfaces (see [The i.AI brand is a corporate
brand and is not applied to product
interfaces](/decisions-record/corporate-brand-not-applied-to-products)).
