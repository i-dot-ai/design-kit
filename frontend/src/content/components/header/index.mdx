## When to use this component

Use the generic header for departmental products, and for products that are not tied
to a specific department (where the department defaults to i.AI). Products are
branded either as GOV.UK or as a government department depending on their audience —
see [Products are branded either GOV.UK or a government
department](/decisions-record/branding-determined-by-audience).

The header is one of the elements that carries the brand — see [Brand is the product
name, header, font, brand colour and
logo](/decisions-record/brand-is-header-font-colour-logo). Set the product name,
logo and brand colour here to brand a departmental product.

The departmental brand is the default. An experimental product, or one whose
audience is not yet settled, starts with the generic header and can switch to the
[GOV.UK header](/components/govuk-header) later.

## When not to use this component

Do not use the generic header for a public-facing or cross-government service. Use
the [GOV.UK header](/components/govuk-header) instead so the product reads as
GOV.UK.

Do not use the generic header to present an i.AI corporate identity. The i.AI brand
is a corporate brand and is not applied to product interfaces — see [The i.AI brand
is a corporate brand and is not applied to product
interfaces](/decisions-record/corporate-brand-not-applied-to-products).
