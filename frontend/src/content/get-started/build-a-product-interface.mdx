If you’re building a new product the [i.AI Cookie Cutter](https://github.com/i-dot-ai/i-dot-ai-cookiecutter) helps you set up the correct project files and integrates the i.AI Design Kit. To do this you need to:

1. [Set up a new project.](#set-up-a-new-project)
2. [Follow the Design Kit guidance.](#follow-the-design-kit-guidance)
3. [Choose a product brand.](#choose-a-product-brand)

## Set up a new project

Follow the [Cookie Cutter instructions to set up a new project](https://github.com/i-dot-ai/i-dot-ai-cookiecutter/blob/main/README.md).

## Follow the Design Kit guidance

Install the community `read-design-kit` skill in OpenCode so your agent follows
the latest Design Kit guidance as you build your product. Find out how to
[install the skills](/get-started/use-the-design-kit-skills).

## Choose a product brand

The Cookie Cutter automatically brands your product with i.AI branding. You can brand your product with:

- i.AI branding
- GOV.UK branding
- a department's branding

Find out how to [choose and apply the right brand for your product](/styles/branding). If you are unsure, use i.AI branding.

## Questions and issues

If you have any issues, contact us on the [#iai-design-kit](https://i-dot-ai.slack.com/archives/C0C476E1D4H) channel.

You can also [suggest or request new content](/get-started/contribute-to-the-design-kit) for the design kit.
