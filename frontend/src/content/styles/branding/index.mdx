The i.AI Design Kit supports the use of different product brands. A brand is defined by a specific header, footer, colour palette and font. Using the Kit, you can brand a product as i.AI, as a department, or as GOV.UK, and switch between them easily.

## Choosing the right brand

Here's how to decide which brand is best for your product:

- **i.AI brand** - Use this if your product is an internal tool for use by i.AI staff, an experiment from i.AI or for material that promotes i.AI as an organisation.
- **Departmental brand** - Use this if your product is for use exclusively by staff from a specific government department.
- **GOV.UK brand** - Use this if your product is part of a public-facing service on GOV.UK, or if it will be used by public servants across multiple organisations.

## How to apply the i.AI brand

To apply the i.AI brand:

- Use the [i.AI header component](/components/header)
- Use the [i.AI footer component](/components/footer)
- Include this line in your Sass file:
  <CodeBlock collection="styles" component="branding" code="./examples/font-styles.scss" />

## How to apply the GOV.UK brand

To apply the GOV.UK brand:

- Use the [GOV.UK header component](/components/govuk-header)
- Use the [GOV.UK footer component](/components/govuk-footer)

For more guidance, see the Service Manual guide; [How to make your service look like GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/making-your-service-look-like-govuk).

## How to apply a departmental brand

To apply a departmental brand:

- Use the department's header component
- Use the [GOV.UK footer component](/components/govuk-footer)
